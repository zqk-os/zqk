# ZQK Public Benefit Corporation (PBC) Charter

**Document Purpose**: To provide the specific "Public Benefit Statement" required by Delaware law when filing Articles of Incorporation via Clerky for a Public Benefit Corporation.

---

## 🏛️ Proposed Public Benefit Statement

> "The specific public benefit of ZQK, Inc. is to design, develop, and distribute decentralized, sovereign artificial intelligence infrastructure that enhances human agency. The Corporation shall operate in a manner that fundamentally protects the intellectual property, data privacy, and operational control of its users, ensuring that advanced computational intelligence remains a localized, transparent, and symbiotic tool for creators rather than an extractive, centralized monopoly."

---

## ⚖️ Strategic Rationale

1. **"decentralized, sovereign artificial intelligence infrastructure"**: Defines our exact market category (GraphRAG, Symbiotic Mesh) without boxing us into a specific programming language or current tech trend.
2. **"enhances human agency"**: The core ethical shield. It legally justifies prioritizing user-in-the-loop features (like the Autonomy Inbox) over pure autonomous scale.
3. **"protects the intellectual property, data privacy, and operational control"**: This is the commercial hook. It translates a "moral" benefit into a massive B2B selling point for enterprise CISOs.
4. **"localized, transparent, and symbiotic tool... rather than an extractive, centralized monopoly"**: The Anthropic maneuver. It legally protects the board if they choose to reject an acquisition offer from a company with a history of hostile data extraction.
